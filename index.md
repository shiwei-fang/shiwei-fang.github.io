### Hello!

My name is **Shiwei Fang** and I am currently an Assistant Professor in the [School of Computer and Cyber Sciences](https://www.augusta.edu/ccs/) at [Augusta University](https://www.augusta.edu/). Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate working with [Prof. Deepak Ganesan](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~dganesan/) in the [College of Information & Computer Sciences](https://www.cics.umass.edu/) at the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://www.umass.edu/). I received my Ph.D. in [Computer Science](https://cs.unc.edu/) from [the University of North Carolina (UNC) at Chapel Hill](https://www.unc.edu/), advised by [Prof. Shahriar Nirjon](https://cs.unc.edu/~nirjon/). Prior to my Ph.D., I received B.E. in Computer Engineering from [Stony Brook University](https://www.stonybrook.edu/).

My research interest falls under the broad umbrella of Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS), and is driven by the goal of enhancing the sensing and computation capability of low-power, low-cost, and low-maintenance ubiquitous systems that have applications in health, public safety, automation, and beyond.

In contrast to developing algorithms based on existing datasets, I look at systems in a pragmatic way, i.e., I build real systems to collect real-world data, examine their characteristics, and push the boundaries of their sensing and computation capabilities.
